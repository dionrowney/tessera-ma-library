# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- The tile is now a key with a title of your own. A tap opens a full-screen list to choose from; a tap on a name plays it.
- Each key opens one list: playlists, artists, radio stations or podcasts. Choose it in the tile's **List** option, which replaces **Opens on**: set it again on a tile you already placed.
- The tile can be as small as one cell.
- The Music Assistant config entry id is now filled in once for the screen instead of on every tile. After this update, enter it in the plugin's details on the screen's Plugins tab and press **Save and build**.

## 0.2.0
- The list fills the tile: up to twelve rows on a large tile, with the room left over shared between the rows.
- A page is full even when Music Assistant's answer is too long for one message, and the next-page key stays.

## 0.1.0
- First version: a tile with your Music Assistant playlists, artists and radio stations; tap one to play it.
- Needs the Music Assistant config entry id in the tile's options.
